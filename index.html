CH-01FIBER OPTICREV 2026

Fiber Optic Installation, Splicing, Testing & Maintenance

Advanced Networks Cabling Services (ANCS) delivers comprehensive fiber optic solutions for commercial, multi-family, enterprise and telecommunications networks throughout Florida. Our experienced technicians specialize in both Inside Plant (ISP) and Outside Plant (OSP) fiber infrastructure, providing reliable, high-performance connectivity from installation through ongoing maintenance.

We have proudly partnered with leading telecommunications providers, including Hotwire Communications, FPL FiberNet, Crown Castle, Comcast, and other industry leaders, supporting projects of all sizes with precision and professionalism.

  • Fiber optic installation and construction
  • Fusion splicing and termination
  • Fiber testing and certification (OTDR & power meter)
  • ISP and OSP fiber deployment
  • Fiber to the Home (FTTH) installations
  • MDF and IDF fiber backbone connectivity
  • Fiber repairs, emergency restoration, and maintenance
  • Network upgrades, relocations, and expansions
  • Fiber troubleshooting and fault isolation

Whether you're expanding an existing network, building new fiber infrastructure, or maintaining mission-critical telecommunications systems, ANCS has the expertise, equipment and skilled workforce to deliver dependable solutions that keep your network operating at peak performance.

CH-04ACCESS CONTROLREV 2026

Modern Access Control Systems

Take control of your facility's security with a modern access control system designed for convenience, visibility and peace of mind. ANCS installs scalable solutions that help protect your property while giving you complete control over who has access to your building. Our systems offer:

  • Secure access using key fobs, mobile credentials, PIN codes or facial recognition
  • Remote management and real-time monitoring from anywhere
  • Employee time and attendance integration
  • Custom access permissions for restricted areas
  • Detailed entry and activity reporting
  • Easy credential management without the need to replace locks or reissue keys

Whether you manage an office, apartment community, warehouse, or commercial facility, ANCS delivers reliable access control solutions tailored to your security needs.
